Sound effects in this folder come from Kenney's packs (www.kenney.nl), all
Creative Commons Zero (CC0): Sci-Fi Sounds, Impact Sounds, UI Audio,
Interface Sounds, Casino Audio and Music Jingles. File names keep the packs'
own names; each is converted to Ogg Vorbis and MP3 (mono, except the jingles).
Which sound plays when is src/audio/sfx.ts (BANK).

boom011_short and boom011_long are cut from EffectiveTrailer_Booms_Vol2_011
(Federico Soler, Effective Trailer Booms Vol. 2) in the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC
2026 bundle: royalty-free, commercial use without attribution, may not be sold
as they come or used to train AI. Cut from the first transient (0.6 s and
1.4 s, faded out), stereo (the source's channels partly cancel in mono),
levelled to -20 LUFS.

Also from the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC 2026 bundle, under the same terms:
boom011_thump (boom 011, low-passed at 800 Hz, 0.6 s); woosh_debris ("Woosh
Debris", Cinematic Sound Design - Colossal Impacts, 0.7 s); cartoon_bubbles
("Cartoon Bubbles Short", 0.48 s); tension_drone_loop ("magic, drone, tension,
spellbound, evanescence-002", 3 s from 0.63 s, its last 0.4 s crossfaded into
its start so it loops without a seam). All stereo, -20 LUFS.
