PLUNDERLAND: PRIVACY

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who runs it

Plunderland is run by TTFN. Contact: nick.ttfn@gmail.com.

No accounts, no cookies, no ads

You don't sign up, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

What we keep, and why

  • A player id: a random code our server gives you the first time you play, along with a secret key your browser keeps so you come back as the same player. It keeps your stats together and isn't linked to who you are.
  • The name you choose. Other players see it during play and on the in-game leaderboard. A name containing a profanity is replaced with a random callsign.
  • Your stats: runs played, time played, loot, kills and the day of your first run, stored with your player id on our game server in the EU. These stats (without names) are published on the game's public stats page.
  • Weekly seasons. Each week we keep, with your player id, your runs, extractions, loot banked and XP for that season, and the name you played your latest run of the week under, in our database in the EU (Railway). The top ten names, with their player ids and banked loot, are shown publicly on the season board (the game server's /season page), and anyone paid at the end of a season has their place and payout kept. Because the board shows player ids, a name on it can be matched to that id's stats on the stats page.
  • Gameplay analytics. When a run starts and ends, our server tells Google Analytics: robot, run number, days since your first run, players and bots in the world, friends who joined through your invite, time to first loot, how the run ended, its length, loot, kills and deepest layer. They're keyed by your player id and collected in Google's EU region. Your browser never contacts Google.
  • Error reports. If the game crashes, a report goes to Sentry (servers in Germany): the error, the browser and operating system, and the game version. We've turned off collection of IP addresses, cookies and request headers.
  • Connections. Our server sees your IP address to connect you but doesn't store it. Our hosting providers keep standard access logs.

In your browser

Local storage holds a secret key that keeps you the same player, an older browser id the game still sends for now, your name, robot, colours, which loadout you last picked for each robot, which season result you have already seen, and a random invite code (it's in the links you share, and isn't linked to your player id); session storage holds a counter used when the game updates itself and the invite you opened the game with. Clearing this site's data makes you a new player: your level, unlocks and season entries stay with the old one, and there is no way to get them back from this browser.

Who processes it

Cloudflare (the website), Railway (the game server, Amsterdam), Google Analytics, and Sentry.

How long

Stats until you ask us to delete them. Season entries, payouts and the name kept with them: until you ask us to delete them. Analytics: 14 months. Error reports: Sentry's retention for our plan.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data. Email us your player name and roughly when you played, and we'll find and delete it.

Children

We don't ask anyone for personal information, including children.
